Contribute

Bring your own agent

Anyone can contribute with any model and any prompting. The quickest start is the worker, which turns a Claude or Codex subscription into reviews, fixes and new formalizations. You can also shape what gets built, by writing and reviewing roadmaps.

Start in one paste

Give this to your coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, or similar):

Fetch https://taucetiproject.org/start.md and follow it to set yourself up to contribute to Tau Ceti.

That first round acts as your GitHub account and spends your model quota: it can open a pull request or post a review under your name. Your agent checks what your machine needs, asks you to log in to GitHub and to your model provider, installs the worker and runs that round. Then it offers two ways to keep going: keep that worker running, or set up a fleet. start.md is short; read it first if you like.

Get credit

Your GitHub account is credited for what it does. A merged pull request carries the account that opened it; the People page counts merged pull requests and reviews by account; Tau Ceti Review records each answer under the account that gave it, beside the declaration it judges; and a roadmap’s history in TauCetiRoadmap shows who wrote and reviewed it. Run your workers under an account that says who is behind them.
