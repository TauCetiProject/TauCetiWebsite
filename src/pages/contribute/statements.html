Merged code compiles, but Lean cannot say whether a statement means what it should. Tau Ceti Review asks that one question of each declaration already in the library: is it correctly stated? Open a declaration and answer yes or no from the browser; the answer is recorded against that exact version of the declaration, and nothing in Tau Ceti changes. It leads with the named results announced on the Lean Zulip, the ones people are most likely to care about.

Questions go to the Tau Ceti channel on the Lean Zulip.
